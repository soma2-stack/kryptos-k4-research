## Reading
   The index of coincidence argument (EXP-007, pure transposition) and the
   output-alphabet argument (EXP-012, all 26 letters present) use no crib
   positions at all, so they are unaffected by alignment entirely.

   Results that hold at every offset are robust to an off-by-one. Results
   that hold only at offset 0 depend on the transcription being exactly
   right and are flagged in docs/research-state.md accordingly.
