## Reading
   A running key read off natural text predicts that ONE convention should
   show clearly text-like key letters while the rest look uniform. That is a
   sharp, pre-registered signature and it is ABSENT: no convention survives
   correction for having tested 24 of them.

   Grade: HEURISTIC NEGATIVE for the natural-language running-key class.
   NOT an elimination. At n=24 the test cannot separate an English sample
   from a uniform one reliably, as the calibration above shows, and a key
   read off PROPER NOUNS - city names on a clock drum - need not follow
   ordinary English letter frequencies at all. The negative is real but weak,
   and it does not touch keystreams read off a chart, a tableau, or a
   numeric structure rather than prose.
