## Part 2 - block coverage, and why trifid is undecidable
   A 27-cell trifid cube is 26 letters plus one filler in 27 cells:
   27! / 1! = 10^28.0 arrangements.

   d   usable blocks   covered positions   constraint   verdict
   2   11              22                  10^31.1      TESTABLE
   3   7               21                  10^29.7      MARGINAL
   4   4               16                  10^22.6      VACUOUS
   5   2               10                  10^14.1      VACUOUS
   6   2               12                  10^17.0      VACUOUS
   7   2               14                  10^19.8      VACUOUS
   8   2               16                  10^22.6      VACUOUS
   9   1               9                   10^12.7      VACUOUS
   10  0               0                   10^0.0       VACUOUS
   11  1               11                  10^15.6      VACUOUS
   12  0               0                   10^0.0       VACUOUS
   13  0               0                   10^0.0       VACUOUS

   Periods 2 and 3 clear the cube's 10^28.0 of freedom and ARE
   testable; EXP-013 tests them directly. Period 4 and above are vacuous:
   do not search them, since any fit found there would be meaningless.

## General lesson
   Crib evidence does not survive block boundaries. A cipher with block
   size d keeps only the blocks lying wholly inside a crib, so 24 known
   letters can fall to 9 or 0 usable constraints. Before searching any
   block-structured cipher, count the covered positions first.

   One filler symbol would be expected to appear about 97/27 = 3.6 times
   in 97 characters; P(a 27th symbol never appears) = 0.026.
   K4 is pure letters, so trifid additionally requires that a 1-in-40
   event occurred. Suggestive against it, but not decisive.
