## Reading
   Encryption direction: at least THREE alphabets are forced. Any model
   that assigns one of two charts per position - including a binary
   parity, Morse or rail selector used that way - is eliminated outright.

   Decryption direction: two charts do suffice, but 16,384 of the 16.7M
   possible selectors work. An arbitrary bit-stream separates all ten
   conflicts with probability 1 in 1,024. The inherited observation that
   parity, a Morse phase and rail alternation each 'had no contradictions'
   is therefore worth about 10 bits, and `research-state.md` records that
   multiple Morse phases were searched. A selector family with ~1,000
   members is expected to contain a winner by chance alone.

   These three leads should be treated as explained, not as anomalies,
   unless a selector is specified in advance and works without phase search.
