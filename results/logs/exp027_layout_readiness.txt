   Tier-1 requires a COMPLETE face, not 97 attested letters. A partially read
   band yields a tape with UNKNOWN GAPS at unknown positions, which cannot be
   used as a keystream at all. The modern lower band holds 9 names; the
   historical one holds an unknown number of which 4 are legible. Until the
   full membership AND order of the historical lower band are known, the face
   is not complete and Tier-1 is NOT met - however close the letter count gets.

   Actual modern total: 146. UNEXPLAINED: 46 names.
   So ~46 names entered at some time OTHER than 1997. If any entered between
   1969 and 1997, the clock was still growing during the target period, and a
   MID-1970s photograph is not evidence for 1989 without demonstrated
   stability. Source 3's nine-name UTC+1 upper band may be an earlier, smaller
   state of that face. Casablanca - the one modern-upper name absent from it -
   is consistent with a later addition, though it could simply be obscured.
