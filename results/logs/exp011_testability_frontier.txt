## How much more plaintext would each class need?
   n crib letters supply n*log10(26) of budget. Requiring a margin of 2
   decades below the parameter count, the letters needed are:

   Vigenere family, fixed alphabet, period 26    needs 28 letters (4 more)
   Quagmire I + period 8                         needs 29 letters (5 more)
   Quagmire I + period 12                        needs 33 letters (9 more)
   Quagmire III + period 8                       needs 48 letters (24 more)
   Affine transposition + Quagmire I period 8    needs 32 letters (8 more)
   Homophonic, 2 charts + free selector          needs 60 letters (36 more)

   This is the most actionable number in the repository. Every class above
   becomes decidable with between 5 and 25 more known plaintext letters.
   Acquiring constraint is worth more than any further search: one more
   released clue would re-open families that are currently unfalsifiable.

## Consequences
   1. Quagmire I is closed. Periods 1-7 are eliminated outright by Part 1,
      and from period 6 upward the class is already vacuous by Part 2.
      There is no period at which it is both possible and testable.
   2. Quagmire III, any two-keyed-alphabet scheme, and any model with a
      free per-position selector are unfalsifiable with 24 crib letters.
      Searching them can only produce fits, never evidence. This is why
      the inherited two-chart lead could not fail: see EXP-009.
   3. Bifid and trifid sit near the frontier: a 25-cell Polybius square
      plus a period is 10^25.2 against a 10^34 budget, so the class is
      TESTABLE in principle. It is the largest classical family left that
      the cribs can actually decide, and it has not been tested here.
   4. Anything combining a free transposition with a free keyed alphabet
      is vacuous. Transposition work must fix the alphabet in advance.
