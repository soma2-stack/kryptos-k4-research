## Why the modern list cannot be substituted - measured, not asserted
   20 of 146 modern names were added in 1997, so 126 are pre-1997.
   A 97-letter keystream window covers roughly 97/L names for mean name
   length L. Probability that such a window contains NO post-1997 addition,
   under a hypergeometric draw without replacement:

   mean name length L   names per window   P(window free of 1997 additions)
        6                   16                    0.082
        7                   14                    0.114
        8                   12                    0.158
        9                   11                    0.186

   So roughly 85-90% of windows of the MODERN tape are contaminated by at
   least one name that did not exist when Sanborn was working. Testing the
   modern list as a proxy would be wrong most of the time, and a negative
   from it would carry almost no information about the historical clock.
   This is the quantitative justification for not taking the shortcut.
